# HackGraph improvements — 7 September 2026 UTC

The local build now supports readable, interruptible graph exploration and explicit evidence status. The warm visual identity remains intact. A subsequent defensive review corrected citation metadata and clarified the graph's modeling conventions.

## Interaction and motion

- Topic lists adjust around the expansion origin at tablet and landscape sizes. Resizing preserves the existing focused choice and keeps it visible in the list. Returning keyboard focus to a card dismisses Help and Settings. Forty-two generic states and two actual-app expansions passed focused-card obstruction checks; the paired performance run records an additional 1–6 ms of setup time in its measured groups. See `reports/OVERLAY_REVIEW.md`.

- Crowded captions now wrap inside the column gap and yield to cards or other captions. Inspecting a connection gives its caption priority. Caption dimensions are cached, and explanations remain available in connection details.
- Wrapped breadcrumbs in the details panel have larger click areas, with desktop and mobile regression coverage.

- Camera and layout use one animation loop. Interruptions start from the displayed position, including offset containers and touch gestures.
- Expansion preserves reading scale, keeps the origin in view, and supplies a topic list for branches that cannot fit. Overview has compact cards and an explicit return action.
- Layout defaults to 360 ms; entrance stagger is bounded to 100 ms. Offscreen entrances avoid painting stacks of cards at the parent, unchanged geometry is not rewritten, and label placement avoids SVG geometry reads.
- Expansion batches card and caption sizing, caches fixed UI elements, and creates opacity effects after deciding which entrances are visible. A branch canceled before its first frame disappears immediately, avoiding a stack of exit effects for content that never appeared.
- The sidebar builds its detailed connected-topic list on first disclosure, then reuses it when reopened. A 250-topic expansion avoids constructing 2,000 hidden elements. Topic text stays in DOM text nodes and icons are cloned without reparsing the rows.
- New branches allocate space around established positions. Compatible peer order stays stable, including topics that acquire another parent. Interrupted layouts use displayed coordinates; collapse compacts the released space. Connector lanes avoid crossings caused solely by their old ordering.
- The revised solver eliminates existing-card movement in the six fixed fixtures. Across 144 sequential expansions in ten varied generic graphs, mean movement falls about 82% and peer inversions fall to zero. The layouts average 19% taller with about 5% more crossings; collapse moves cards farther to recover space. The decision report records these tradeoffs and ELK's remaining advantage on dense crossing count.
- Reduced motion responds to preference changes during a session.
